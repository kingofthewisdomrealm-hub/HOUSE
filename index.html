HOUSE

Working models of Treasure Coast houses that still tell the truth. A periodic table of parts, a house you build by hand, and ten county inspectors who each see it once. Training — not a permit.

Start with Block. It teaches the order — one house, one clean path, no choices to make. Block · Advanced comes after, and teaches the judgement: where the job forks, what the county adds when you fork, and what it costs you when you pick wrong.

HOUSE Block 3D The same house as a real model you can spin. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; cutaway is a plane slicing the building in half so you can look inside. Needs WebGL — on a modern phone or laptop, yes. HOUSE Block The flat version. Section and elevation, no 3D. 40 tiles, 10 county gates, two traps — identical rules, runs on anything. HOUSE Block · Advanced The same house, but now you decide. Five forks — foundation, roof covering, air handler, studs, insulation — and the first one changes how many times the county comes out. Pick stem wall and 10 inspections become 12; every step after it renumbers itself. Eight traps, four of which only exist on the road you took. Scored in days and dollars.
